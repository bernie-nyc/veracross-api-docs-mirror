# Support

If you are experiencing access or scope errors, you can review your access token and verify that the correct scopes have been granted to you by the school in the partner portal.

We do not currently model a way for vendors to request scopes access via our Partner portal, so any requests for scopes access should be routed directly to your contact at the school who can grant you access (Veracross employees do not grant vendors access to schools’ data directly).

> Any further questions or requests can be sent to partners@veracross.com.
